# Writer's Assistant

> Where Stories Live

Writer's Assistant is a desktop-first authoring platform designed to help writers create, organize, worldbuild, and publish stories from a single unified workspace.

## Vision

Writer's Assistant provides a virtual Writer's Study containing dedicated tools for:

- Writing manuscripts
- Character management
- Worldbuilding
- Timeline tracking
- Story arc management
- Research organization
- Inspiration management
- Publishing completed works

The platform is designed around the philosphy:

> Write Once. Use Everywhere.

Information written in a manuscript automatically connects to Characters, Locations, Events, Story Arcs, Atlas entries, and Chronicle timelines through the Archivist AI system.

---

# Core Principles

## Author Ownership

Authors retain full ownership of:

- Manuscripts
- Characters
- Locations
- Worldbuilding
- Research
- Published Works

Writer's Assistant does not claim ownership of user-created content.

---

## Tool First

Writer's Assistant is:

- 90% Authoring Platform
- 10% Inspiration Discovery

The platform is not intended to function as a social network.

# Major Systems

## Writing Studio

Manuscript editor.

## Character Archive

Character dossiers, timelines, relationships, and portraits.

## Character Web

Interactive character relationship visualization.

## Atlas

Worldbuilding and location management.

## Chronicle

Story timeline and event tracking.

## Story Arcs

Narrative thread management.

## Inspiration Cabinet

Inspiration collections, research boards, and community discovery.

## Vault

Project archive and storage.

## Published Works

Author-controlled showcase for completed books.

## The Archivist

AI-powered continuity and story management assistant.
